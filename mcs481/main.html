Computational Geometry is devoted to the study of algorithms to solve geometrical problems. The development of the field is driven by applications to computer graphics, computer aided design, motion planning and visibility problems in robotics, geographic information systems, integrated circuit design, etc...

The text book for this course is Computational Geometry. Algorithms and Applications by Mark de Berg, Otfried Cheong, Marc van Kreveld, and Mark Overmars, published by Springer.

We will use the software CGAL to explore the algorithms and applications. Three projects will be assigned during the semester.

Slides for the lectures are posted below. We cover the first eleven chapters in the textbook and skim the topics of the last chapters.
