Course Content

For our introduction to symbolic computation, we will use the free and open source software SageMath.

There is no textbook for this course, lecture notes are available

  • as one single pdf file; or
  • in html format.
The lecture notes are still a work in progress...

As a reference, you could take a look at Sage for Undergraduates written by Gregory V. Bard.
Another reference is the Sage for Power Users written by William Stein.

Slides of the lectures and Jupyter notebooks will be posted below.
